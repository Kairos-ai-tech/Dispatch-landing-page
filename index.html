How it works

One message, sent the way a person would send it.

Bulk tools blast from their servers and shared reputations. Dispatch goes out through your Gmail, one at a time, so every email lands in the Sent folder you already know.

  1. 1

    Connect Gmail

    Approve once. Pick any verified send-as alias as the sender. Disconnect whenever you like and the stored token is revoked.

  2. 2

    Compose

    Paste emails or upload a CSV. Use merge tags like {{firstName}} and {{company}}, add a signature, queue up to four follow-ups.

  3. 3

    Dispatch paces it

    About a minute between messages and a daily cap, so a campaign looks like a person working through a list, not a burst.

  4. 4

    Follow up, then stop

    Follow-ups go out as replies in the same thread. The moment someone answers or unsubscribes, their sequence ends.

Track

See each recipient's journey.

Sent, opened, clicked, replied, unsubscribed. Pause or resume a running campaign, and compare campaigns side by side in Reports.

  • Reply detection keeps follow-ups from landing on top of an answer
  • Click tracking through a redirect you can switch off per campaign
  • Engagement labels in Gmail, opt-in

Compose

Personal at the first line, scalable at the tenth hundred.

Merge fields, per-language signatures, an optional send-at time. Imported campaigns always arrive as drafts, so nothing leaves until you press Send.

Automation

Plug it into whatever already builds your lists.

Generate an API key, then post a CSV from cron, GitHub Actions, Zapier or n8n. The key can only import, so it can't send mail or touch your Gmail connection. Repeat the same campaign name and the rows merge into one draft.

Prefer to stay in the inbox? The Gmail add-on builds and imports campaigns from inside Gmail.

$ curl -X POST "$DISPATCH_URL/api/addon/campaign.import" \
  -H "X-Dispatch-Api-Key: $KEY" \
  -d '{"name": "Daily sync",
       "csv": "email,subject,content,sendAt
ann@example.com,Hi,Hello Ann,09:30"}'

→ draft created · 1 recipient · review, then press Send

Built to stay out of the spam folder.

  • Paced sendingAbout a minute between messages, with a daily cap you control. Overflow rolls to tomorrow.
  • One-click unsubscribeEvery message carries it. Unsubscribes go on a suppression list that applies across all your campaigns.
  • Your reputation, your threadMail leaves from your own address and threads like normal, so replies and the Sent folder stay yours.
  • No blind retriesIf a send's outcome is unclear, Dispatch marks it failed instead of retrying, so nobody gets the same email twice.
